Bee Bracelet Flasher

nRF52840 over SWD, in Chrome. Needs a CMSIS-DAP probe (e.g. a ±$15 DAPLink, or a J-Link) wired to the bracelet's SWD pads: SWDIO, SWCLK, GND, and VTG/VREF to the bracelet's 3V.

Reads all 1 MB of flash to a file. Do this before anything else — it's your undo. If the chip is read-protected (APPROTECT), this fails and the log says so; we then restore from the stock images pulled from the Bee app instead.
